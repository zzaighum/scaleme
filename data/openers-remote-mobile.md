## Three rules for sending these

1. **Re-check the posting is live on the morning you send.** Two of seven
   companies went dead inside nine days. A cold email about a filled role is
   worse than no email.
2. **Never send a placeholder.** If you can't substantiate a rate or a
   timeline, delete the sentence — the sourced half still carries the message.
3. **One email per company**, even where two roles are open. MyFitnessPal gets
   a single email covering both; Life360's other mobile roles are Canada-tagged
   and should not be mentioned.
